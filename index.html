The Story of an Artist

By Hanna Safrea Ducusin

Girl wakes up in her bedroom.

She got out of bed and saw something unusual.

Guitar shaped? Idk.

She approached the figure and discovered it was a guitar.

She picked it up and strummed the strings. The sound was beautiful.

She decided to take it with her and learn how to play it.

She practiced every day and became a skilled guitarist.

She performed in front of an audience and everyone roared with applause.

She realized that music was her true passion and decided to pursue a career in it.

She became a famous musician and inspired many people with her music.

She lived happily ever after, making music and spreading joy to the world.
